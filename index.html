What the hub does

Share resources

Answers that worked, templates and the people who know.

  • Search across every department
  • Confirmed solutions stay findable
  • Word, Teams, SharePoint and more on each profile

Get alerts

Email when something needs you, on the schedule you choose.

  • Replies, tags and connection requests
  • Instant, daily or weekly
  • Campus announcements planned next

Run campus functions

Requests reach the office that owns them, and recurring problems get fixed at the source.

  • Posts reviewed before they go live
  • Routed to the right office
  • Recurring issues elevated for review
